11. Name some forms of communication and why?
Forms of communication include oral, written, non-verbal and visual communication. They help us share information, express ideas, understand others and maintain relationships.

16. Briefly describe some communication tools used for communication.
Mobile phones, email, letters, video conferencing, social media and messaging apps are communication tools. They help people exchange information quickly and effectively.

Part D — Descriptive / Narrative Questions (5 marks each)

18. How did the forms of communication change from ancient to modern age? What does this fact show?
In ancient times, people communicated through gestures, signs, smoke signals, drum beats, messengers and handwritten letters. Communication was slow and limited by distance. Today, people use mobile phones, email, video calls, social media and the internet. These make communication fast and convenient worldwide. This change shows the rapid development of science and technology and the importance of communication in human progress.
19. How do the emotions of a person disturb communication?
Emotions affect communication. Anger may make a person speak rudely; fear may stop them from expressing ideas; stress reduces concentration and listening; sadness and anxiety may reduce confidence and cause confusion. We should stay calm, control emotions and listen patiently to communicate effectively.

21. What is the difference between Resume and Curriculum Vitae (CV)?
A resume is a short, job-focused document highlighting relevant qualifications, skills and experience. A CV is a more detailed record of educational background, achievements, skills and professional history.
